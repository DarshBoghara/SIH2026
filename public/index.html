The Problem We Are Addressing

Early and accurate disease detection significantly improves patient outcomes and reduces overall healthcare expenditure. However, real-world biomedical data is inherently high-dimensional, noisy, heterogeneous, and complex. While classical machine learning methods have achieved notable success, they face mathematical scaling challenges when navigating these high-dimensional biological landscapes.

Protein Data

What it is: High-dimensional proteomic profiles, amino acid sequences, and structural representations.
Why it's hard: Complex conformational states, non-linear folding interactions, and high feature dimensionality.
Why preprocessing matters: Rigorous feature filtering and variance reduction are required to isolate true predictive biomarkers.
HL70.82NaN FHIRNORM124 IMP0.05K-NN

Medical Records

What it is: Longitudinal electronic health records (EHR) spanning labs, diagnoses, and vitals.
Why it's hard: Sparse tables, extensive missing entries, asynchronous timestamps, and mixed categorical data.
Why preprocessing matters: Multi-method imputation and robust normalization are essential to preserve clinical integrity.
DICOM · 512²

Medical Imaging

What it is: High-resolution spatial scans including MRI, CT, histopathology, and radiomic maps.
Why it's hard: High intra-class variability, subtle morphological abnormalities, and computational density.
Why preprocessing matters: Dimension-compact feature extractors must distill structural signatures before modeling.

Clinical Datasets

What it is: Tabular diagnostic panels, demographic parameters, and multi-cohort patient trials.
Why it's hard: Heavy class imbalance where positive disease cases represent a small fraction of the cohort.
Why preprocessing matters: Stratified scaling and rebalancing safeguards models against deceptive majority-class bias.

BioSync proposes investigating quantum-enhanced learning alongside strong classical baselines.

Why a Hybrid Architecture?

Current Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) devices face fundamental hardware constraints in physical qubit count and circuit coherence time. High-dimensional biomedical data cannot be directly encoded onto existing quantum processors without prior compression. BioSync addresses this through a purposeful division of labor.

🖥️
CLASSICAL STAGE

Classical Front-End

High-dimensional data cleaning, imputation, and PCA variance reduction happen classically on standard compute to compress thousands of features down to a compact, quantum-compatible input space.

Quantum Processing

Parameterized quantum circuits (VQC, QNN, QSVM) operate on the compact encoded space, evaluating quantum feature representations and non-linear Hilbert space correlations with shallow circuit depth.

Why VQC fits BioSync:

VQC is proposed as a direct quantum classification approach using trainable circuit parameters. It fits BioSync because it works with a compact encoded feature space, provides a trainable quantum circuit, and is compatible with simulator/NISQ-oriented development. VQC will be evaluated against classical baselines.

Classical Models Are Not Optional

The project aims to benchmark hybrid quantum approaches against established classical approaches.

⚛️

Proposed Quantum Models

  • VQC: Variational circuits with parameterized rotation gates.
  • QNN: Layered quantum transformations with classical loss feedback.
  • QSVM: Quantum inner-product kernel estimation in Hilbert space.
💻

Classical Baselines (Required)

  • SVM: RBF & polynomial kernel baselines on identical features.
  • Random Forest: Non-linear ensemble handling feature interactions.
  • XGBoost: State-of-the-art gradient boosted decision trees.
  • Neural Network: Multi-layer perceptron (MLP) deep architecture.

Challenges & Mitigation

Challenge

Few Qubits / Circuit Depth

Mitigation

PCA dimensionality reduction + mutual-information feature selection to construct compact quantum registers.

Challenge

Simulation Cost

Mitigation

Compact quantum input spaces (4–8 qubits) keeping statevector simulation tractable on classical workstations.

Challenge

Noisy / Missing Data

Mitigation

Multi-step data cleaning + iterative imputation + robust z-score normalization and validation gates.

Challenge

Overfitting / Dataset Bias

Mitigation

Rigorous stratified k-fold cross-validation combined with isolated hold-out test evaluations.

Challenge

Model Interpretability

Mitigation

SHAP integration providing transparent mathematical feature attributions and confidence margin explanations.

Why Architecture Components Matter

SE(3) Equivariance

Preserves spatial symmetry under 3D translation and rotation transformations in molecular space.

Message Passing

Facilitates atom-to-atom and residue-level biological feature updates across the graph network.

Iterative Refinement

Progressively optimizes 3D coordinates across successive layer blocks to minimize structural drift.

Torsional Updates

Directly parameterizes flexible dihedral and rotatable bond angles during ligand conformation.

Curvature Features

Encodes surface curvature to accurately detect complementary receptor binding pocket contours.

Radial Basis Features

Projects inter-atomic Euclidean distances onto continuous Gaussian kernel bases.

Clash Penalty

Enforces steric hindrance constraints to penalize physically impossible atom-atom overlap.

Coordinate Loss

Calculates explicit loss against ground-truth crystallographic coordinates during training.

Affinity Head

Specialized regression head predicting binding affinity free energy values (kcal/mol).

Mish Activation

Smooth, non-monotonic activation function preventing gradient saturation in deep layers.

These architectural components are reported to affect performance when removed or modified.
