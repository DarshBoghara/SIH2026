## 🔬 Executive Overview

**BioSync** is an advanced biomedical intelligence prototype proposed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement 26139)**. It addresses the mathematical scaling bottlenecks of classical machine learning when processing high-dimensional, noisy multi-omic sequencing data and clinical streams.

By uniting **classical preprocessing**, **quantum-enhanced feature space mapping (VQC, QNN, QSVM)**, **explainable AI (SHAP)**, and **in-silico molecular docking benchmarks**, BioSync bridges the gap between raw biological complexity and clinical decision support.

---

## ⚡ Key System Components

1. **Multi-Omic & Clinical Data Ingestion**:
   - Genomic variant sequencing profiles, electronic health records (EHR/FHIR/HL7), clinical imaging radar, and longitudinal proteomics.
2. **Classical Preprocessing Pipeline**:
   - Statistical imputation, variance thresholding, and dimensionality reduction down to compact feature representations.
3. **Quantum Machine Learning Layer**:
   - **VQC (Variational Quantum Classifier)**: 4-qubit parameterized ansatz with $RY(x)$ feature encoding and entangling CNOT gates.
   - **QNN (Quantum Neural Network)**: Deep layered variational neural weights $W(\theta)$ with expectation value readout.
   - **QSVM (Quantum Support Vector Machine)**: Non-linear kernel estimation computing inner products in Hilbert space.
4. **Explainable AI (XAI) & SHAP Transparency**:
   - Kernel SHAP attribution waterfall metrics providing transparent feature attribution for clinicians.
5. **Molecular Docking Benchmarks**:
   - Structural folding and binding pocket evaluations across crystallographic targets (KRAS, EGFR, TP53, PDB: 1YKR, PDB: 4KD1).
